## 8. What a CEO session needs to open tickets for a project

- Goals: `governance/strategy.md` (gap 0.1) and `company.yaml:projects[]` (class, share, kill_criteria). Nothing tells the CEO to read `company.yaml` (readable; only write-locked) nor that project ids come from there.
- Reading the project: CEO has `[read]`, cwd = live company checkout; projects are at `projects[].path` (relative, e.g. `../app`). Say so in `org/roles/ceo.md`, and tell it to read the project's `AGENTS.md`/`README`, open PRs on `t/*` branches, and prior tickets via `ticket_list` to form a backlog. Without `web` it cannot read GitHub issues; either grant `web` or say the Operator's issues arrive as `holdco ticket new`.
- Ticket format: `ticket-lifecycle.md:38-48` minus `id`; add `scope_paths` (optional) and the rule "`action_type` is the completing action, owned by `department`, present in `company.yaml:actions`". Give one worked example in `ceo.md` (engineering feature → `code.merge.app`, p1, project `app`, three checkable `done_criteria`).
- Proposal format: `approvals.md` §2 (decision, goal line, alternatives rejected, cost, rollback plan) and §5 inbox template (Title, Recommendation, Why now, Evidence, Risk, Reversibility, Cost, Default) are two field sets with no schema. Define `Proposal` in `protocols/proposal.schema.md` + `src/protocol/proposal.ts`: `ticket`, `action_type`, `title`, `decision`, `strategy_ref`, `alternatives[]`, `cost {usd, agent_hours}`, `rollback_plan`, `reversible_until`, `risk`, `why_now`, `evidence[]`, `urgent: bool` (+reason). The kernel renders the inbox item from it. Define `Review`: `proposal_id`, `outcome: concur|object|needs-info`, `failed_checks: [1-5]`, `reason` (≤1 paragraph), `missing_fact` (needs-info only; once per proposal). State that the COO receives `decision, cost, rollback_plan, reversible_until` plus policy and metrics, never `strategy_ref` reasoning.
- Also tell the CEO: `blocked` tickets are its queue each cycle (`ticket_unblock` → `triaged` or `declined`); `declined` needs a reason; T2 proposals without a COO go straight to the Operator; the digest narrative is the only `report_write` it does.
